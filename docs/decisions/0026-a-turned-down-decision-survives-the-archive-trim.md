# 0026 — A decision the gate turned down survives the archive trim

## Context

`0018` made the archive entry a receipt by deleting every section the close had already harvested,
`## Decisions` among them, on the ground that what found no durable home is the only residue worth
carrying — which is why `## Notes` was its one exception. It named its own reopening trigger as a
session reaching for a deleted section.

The decision gate then grew two outcomes that promote nothing: a candidate that failed a named leg,
and one no leg settled. Under `0018` both were written into the doc at close and deleted from it in
the same step, so the third arrival of an idea read as a fresh question rather than a pattern.

## Decision

The trim spares two line classes inside `## Decisions` — `(rejected: <leg>)` and
`(unsettled: <what would settle it>)` — and deletes the rest of the section along with `## Goal`,
`## Out of scope`, `## Anchors` and `## References`. Everything else `0018` decided stands: the entry
is history and not a promotion target, `## Notes` keeps only what no durable target took, and
`## Why rejected` still survives on a rejected change. A turned-down decision qualifies on `0018`'s
own ground — it is residue no durable layer will accept.

## Trade-off

The receipt grows by the one class of reasoning `0018` deliberately dropped, and an entry is now
inconsistent about why some thinking survives and most does not. Keeping the whole section was the
cheaper rule to state and is what `0018` chose; it loses because a turned-down candidate has nowhere
else to live, where a settled one is already in this folder. A separate rejected-decisions log was
rejected too: a second file per change, read by nobody, for a few lines a year.

This record exists because `0018` was contradicted, not because the change cleared the bar — undoing
it is four prose edits and no migration. The alternative was leaving an active record asserting a
delete the skill no longer performs, which is the failure this whole change was written to stop.

## Revisit when

A receipt's spared lines outgrow its task list, or a rejected candidate is re-nominated with its
earlier `(rejected:)` line sitting unread in the archive — which would mean the pattern needs a queue
rather than a receipt.
